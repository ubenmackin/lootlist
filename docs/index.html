How It Works

Built for real allowance — not fantasy currency

SwiftData is the instant UI source of truth and CloudKit is the cloud system of record. Every payout is real currency, allocated — never subtracted.

✅

Chores & Approvals

Quests use Auto-Approve or Parent Verify per template or per Quest. Completions move through pending → verified / rejected / withdrawn and parents are notified on pending.

🪣

Buckets

Exactly three — Spend / Short Term Save / Long Term Save. Split percentages apply to future payouts and deposits only; existing balances never rebalance retroactively.

🎯

Goals & FIFO

Goals fill FIFO inside their bucket: the oldest incomplete, non-archived goal fills first, overflow cascades to the next, and surplus past all goals stays unallocated. Progress and pacing at a glance.

📅

Allowance & Payouts

WeekMath owns every week as a payout-day-aware half-open [start, end). AllowancePeriod .paid is the atomic skip-guard against double runs; realTime settling credits completions immediately, period closure happens only in runPayout.

🏆

Trophy Room

Achievements from quest-count tiers plus goal-based milestones — including First Goal Created and Goal Getter — plus streak badges. No XP or levels are shown; requirement text is computed at render time.

🔒

Private iCloud Sync

CloudKit Shared Database via CKSyncEngine. Server → cache writes ride BackgroundCacheActor ingest with single-save batches. Shares use .none publicPermission — families join only by explicit readWrite invite, roles decoded from the share title.

Family Roles

Three roles. One shared zone.

Role drives which database every operation targets — and which privileged mutations are allowed. Sensitive checks re-resolve the owner fresh every time.

👑

Guild Master

Owner · Private Database

The family creator. Holds the private zone and the server-stamped Family.creatorUserRecordName owner anchor.

  • Owner-anchored (irreversible): deleteFamilyAndReset, updateMemberRole, kickMember — require isFamilyOwner, deny-by-default when unresolved
  • Reversible owner OR parent: updateFamilyName, updatePayoutDay, updatePayoutPolicy
  • Mints role-targeted invites (Hero / Co-Parent) with .none publicPermission
🛡️

Ranger

Co-Parent · Shared Database

Trusted co-parent with shared-database access. Can manage day-to-day policy without holding the owner anchor.

  • Allowed: updateFamilyName, updatePayoutDay, updatePayoutPolicy (owner OR parent)
  • Allowed: Quest verification, payout finalization, interest/match configuration, ledger export/import (parent role)
  • Not allowed: deleteFamilyAndReset, updateMemberRole, kickMember — owner anchor only
⚔️

Hero

Participant · Shared Database

Participant view focused on progress — complete Quests, allocate to buckets, and fill FIFO goals. No privileged family mutations.

  • Complete Quests and track approval state (pending / verified / rejected / withdrawn)
  • Manage own buckets and goals; unallocated surplus stays in the bucket
  • Earn Trophy Room achievements and streak badges — no XP or level display
